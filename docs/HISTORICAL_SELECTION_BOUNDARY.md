# Historical selection and forward monitoring

[Project](../README.md) · [Research protocol](RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md)

The 2026–2027 season is a monitoring period. Its outcomes do not select a champion, rank candidates for promotion, tune a threshold, fit calibration parameters, or adjust ensemble weights.

## Two independent records

The selection record contains a predeclared candidate set, exact historical cohorts, chronological model origins, source identities, historical scores, uncertainty, and a deterministic decision rule. It is frozen before forward evaluation. The monitoring record applies that frozen choice to later observations and keeps every failure visible.

A successful run is not a promotion. An improved monitoring score is not evidence that the same candidate would have been chosen before the season. Research designs created after inspecting the monitoring period remain exploratory; confirmation requires genuinely later, untouched decisions.

## What the public example verifies

The educational selection example accepts only declared historical seasons. It rejects later rows, missing or duplicate candidate/season records, different cohort identities, ambiguous update protocols, and invalid metrics. Its demonstration uses invented aggregate scores. It neither reproduces the private football model-selection rule nor chooses a live champion.

The separate paper-decision example freezes a complete candidate universe before its native league-week lock. Selection does not accept outcomes. Settlement occurs through a different operation, and missing outcomes remain missing. Display prices, execution assumptions, fees, and observed fills have distinct meanings.

## Reproducibility boundary

The public artifacts contain generic mechanisms and sanitized aggregate research evidence. Individual forecasts, raw licensed observations, active feature transformations, production weights, and private execution configuration remain excluded. Historical publication-time gaps remain disclosed even when a downloaded source table is complete.

Passing these checks demonstrates specific engineering properties. It does not establish a profitable sports strategy, prospective model superiority, or a complete production deployment.
