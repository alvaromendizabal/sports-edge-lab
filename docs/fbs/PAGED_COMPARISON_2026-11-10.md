## The failure this continuation addresses

A large frozen comparison may require several bounded pages. Finishing one page does not establish that the full declared candidate population is present. Comparing only the most recently visited page can also lose a strong candidate from an earlier page. Repeatedly restarting at the same prefix can use the budget without extending research coverage.

The continuation is being prepared to verify the complete page inventory, reuse eligible exact saved work, and carry completed candidate comparisons across resumptions. Final selection must wait for the required historical support and complete comparison scope. Current-season outcomes remain outside historical champion selection.
