# FBS publication boundary and evaluation method

This page applies to the FBS dashboard. The shared repository publication policy continues to apply. Earlier published FBS evidence remains an immutable, dated legacy record; its continued availability does not qualify it for a new purpose.

## Public and private material

The public dashboard may show verified fixture and result observations, aggregate evaluation and coverage, safe model-family labels, source dates, forecast-age metadata, explicit missingness and broad methodology. Newly recovered complete **unissued numerical forecast grids** remain private, along with fitted states, exact active recipes, transformations, weights, cloud identifiers, credentials and licensed raw provider archives. A target being available privately does not mean it is an authenticated public issue.

Earlier public numerical assets are preserved byte for byte as dated controls. New result observations do not overwrite their original forecast values, fitted-state identities, cutoff clocks, evaluation cohorts or failure records. A resolved exact kickoff creates a new fixture epoch; forecasts from another epoch are not rebound.

## Four evidence classes

| Class | What would qualify | State in this report |
|---|---|---|
| Original frozen issued forecast | Original immutable prediction and independent pregame issuance evidence | Zero authenticated issues represented |
| Historical rolling reconstruction | Earlier-data reconstruction with a declared origin and availability limits | Original fixed-recipe controls and separate changed-input studies are retained; this does not establish nested rolling selection |
| Genuine future-issued rolling forecast | Registered earlier-origin selection, admitted inputs, compatible fitted snapshot and independent pregame issue | Pending owner execution and evidence |
| Source-only reference | Exact provider/product/game identity and stated source/cutoff clocks | Four provider views stay separate; incomplete historical matrices and qualification limits remain explicit |

## Selection, training and forecasting

A candidate recipe, champion-selection policy, fitted snapshot and issued forecast are different records. The V4 challenger starts from prior completed-season evidence and compares registered history-window policies. At each origin it may use only admitted earlier labels and features. Training, preprocessing, selection, calibration and combination each respect that origin. Earlier-origin predictions are required for calibration and combinations; in-sample substitutes do not qualify.

Changed admitted inputs require a new fitted snapshot under the registered policy. Exact compatible saved work is reused, not rerun to inflate counts. Calibration may be retained only with a dated justification. Feature refresh and parameter refit are separately recorded. Long-range outlooks keep their original horizons and require a later eligible refresh as kickoff approaches. A new website publication is neither a source refresh nor a model refit.

The selected fixed blend is a **legacy comparison control**. It is preserved for attribution and reproducibility; it is not automatically the permanent production champion. Historical development evidence remains exposed development. A monitored current-season result does not retroactively authorize tuning or promotion.

## Coverage and unresolved work

Every identified fixture remains visible. Target gaps distinguish insufficient history, missing output, unresolved kickoff, a new fixture epoch, protected interrupted work, outside study scope and source/cutoff failure. Numeric zero is never a substitute for a missing metric or probability.

Feature counts distinguish proposed, materialized, preflighted, admitted, evaluated and promoted definitions. AP, Coaches and CFP editions are separate; an unlisted team is not automatically rank 26. Public data-range documentation does not prove a returned row, an account entitlement or original historical availability. Injury reports do not establish player contribution, expected snaps, starter status or replacement quality without additional qualified evidence.

Probability evaluation uses exact target/game cohorts and proper losses. Point evaluation includes errors and saved distribution diagnostics where present. Exact-line cover probability requires an admitted line, product, settlement, cutoff and conditional-distribution join. The displayed report contains zero qualified cover outputs. Provider quotes and quoted point intervals are not interchangeable with model score expectations.

## Source and deployment proof

Public source is restricted to FBS paths, reviewed and tested at an exact Git head, merged without bypassing required checks, and exported from that merged tree. The existing Site keeps its audience. Each published asset receives a byte count and SHA-256 in the publication manifest. Any incomplete served-byte or archived-source verification is disclosed in the publication handoff.
