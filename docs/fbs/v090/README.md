## Selection and metric availability

2024 and 2025 are validation. The default selection score is the equal average of their annual metrics. The four additional ratios—40/60, 30/70, 20/80 and 10/90—are sensitivity views of the exact same predictions. No season-based training weights or sensitivity refits are introduced. The report preserves all 45 saved objective/profile leaders and all 5,955 candidate rank entries.

Some raw point forecasts do not have a predictive distribution. **136 margin/total policies have unavailable CRPS values**, represented by 680 unranked entries across the five ratios. They remain in the comparison with a missing metric and the full expected population. Missing values are not rank zero, perfect scores or zero-filled predictions. Point-error metrics and CRPS each display their actual metric numerator.

The new 50/50 leaders come from LightGBM, CatBoost and XGBoost. All nine primary objective identities change from the prior market study, but eight of those leaders have worse exposed-2026 results than the preceding leaders. Only winner Brier improves. These test observations do not change the validation-selected identities and did not define the next campaign. Calibration also has negative findings: the selected winner family's equal-year validation log loss is better raw than calibrated.

All results remain **frozen reconstructions**, with prior 2026 exposure disclosed. They do not establish original pregame possession, genuine historical issuance, an untouched confirmation test or production promotion.
