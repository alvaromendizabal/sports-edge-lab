What would establish a stronger claim?

A separately registered rolling policy; verified historical source possession; pre-cutoff immutable issues; genuinely unseen prospective evaluation; exact matched provider products and quotes; executable depth, fees and settlement; uncertainty intervals that reflect weeks and repeated research. More fits or a polished dashboard do not substitute for these.

What this public demo does and does not reproduce

It reproduces invented information-time gates, result-union behavior, immutable issue handling, basic metric arithmetic, and partial-owner accounting. It does not reproduce licensed datasets, proprietary estimators, the complete private modeling study, current game picks, or a live betting system. Browser interaction logic and HTML semantics are tested; this is not a claim of a complete external accessibility audit.
