## What the engineering evidence establishes

The owner passed 2,856 checks and completed 24 fits in 24 attempts. Supervised runtime was 576.018 seconds, with peak process-tree memory of 4.135 GB. These are observed measurements on one workload, not hardware-independent performance claims. Independent saved-output rescoring matched 2,046 probability scorecards within 2.23×10⁻¹⁶ and recomputed 32 matched MARKET uncertainty intervals.

The recovered public work now includes an outcome-free historical selector, exact serving-lineage contracts, a persisted paper portfolio and transactional prospective decision/settlement examples. They use invented fixtures, make no live orders and do not reproduce the private models. Basketball B003 evidence, its notebook and CI checks remain preserved on the same repository.
