What information does each model use?

Feature families explain the experiment; private formulas and fitted weights remain outside the public artifact.

SystemInformation familiesInterpretation
Raw MARKETComplete-book paired moneyline consensus; no project football features.Bookmakers’ internal feature sets are unknown.
Learned MARKET modelsPer-book quotes, missingness, book identity and archived price paths.A learned probability model; distinct from raw MARKET and executable quotes.
C: compact football + MARKETTeam history, offense/defense, opponent-adjusted opportunity, matchup, feedback, dynamic form and data quality.Temporal releases and cohort eligibility govern every input.
L: C + learned footballLearned offense/defense opportunity representations, recent form, matchup, drift, uncertainty and support.Generating-model vintages remain part of feature availability.
U: C + direct footballAdditional direct player/team participation and contextual football measurements.More measurements can add noise, redundancy and shift.
UL: combined experimentC, direct football and learned representations evaluated together in all four native frameworks.Both historical gates failed; this is not the current champion.
Retained referencesMetric- and cohort-specific football + MARKET or learned MARKET models.Selected exposed leaders within this project, not a global leaderboard.

Kalshi is a separate execution venue with contract-specific prices, fees and settlement rules. It is not the raw MARKET benchmark displayed above.

An explicit reproducibility boundary.

Public and executable

Chronological synthetic training, proper scoring, identity and time guards, integrity-checked artifacts, tests, notebooks and these sanitized aggregate results.

Private research assets

Licensed observations, individual forecasts, fitted production weights, exact active feature transforms, tuning and decision configuration. The public example does not reproduce the private scores.
