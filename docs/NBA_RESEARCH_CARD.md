## Experiment card: identify the source of a change

Changing features and training windows together confounds their effects. The primary B010 NBA comparison registers every cell below before interpreting a result.

| Representation | Original training window | Revised training window |
|---|---|---|
| Incumbent features | Original control | Window-only contrast |
| Candidate features | Feature-only contrast | Combined change |

Each arm uses both ending-2025 and ending-2026 validation seasons. The validation populations are pinned and identical within each season across all four arms. Four arms × two seasons means eight registered jobs, not eight executed native units or the full size of the broader study. A job may include preprocessing, outcome heads and calibration components, which require separate accounting.

Chronological earlier training and inner-validation information govern fitting and model choices. Both reported historical validation seasons are already development-exposed. Current 2026–2027 outcomes cannot select features, windows, calibrators, ensembles, thresholds or a champion. Current-season monitoring and prospective settlement remain separate evidence products.

Report season results separately, with probability calibration, point/margin/total errors and exact game support. Any MARKET comparison must use the same games and decision cutoffs, show paired uncertainty and retain the existing qualification gates. More recovered prices improve coverage; they do not by themselves improve predictive performance.

## Source card: cached evidence still needs qualification

| Source family | Admission evidence | Current limitation |

| Schedules and game identities | NBA league, provider, native ID, event time and season | Provider-covered schedules are not automatically a certified league denominator |
| Team statistics and historical boxes | Exact raw revision, validated definitions and a declared availability contract | Incomplete or inconsistent boxes stay explicit; a repair candidate is not a canonical repair |
| Player, lineup and injury information | Historical publication/observation clocks and valid pregame joins | Joined records do not prove historically admissible player features |
| MARKET observations | Exact event/market identity, quote and snapshot clocks, freshness, source rights | A later closing quote cannot replace a missing historical cutoff observation |
| Shared raw archives | Verified content digest and original request/response provenance | A cache hit is neither provider consent nor proof of cutoff eligibility |

The public strict timing example requires the exact version to have been published and observed before cutoff. A historical release-lag assumption remains labeled reconstructed research. Retrieval time cannot substitute for original availability, and final lineups or postgame statistics cannot enter an earlier forecast.
