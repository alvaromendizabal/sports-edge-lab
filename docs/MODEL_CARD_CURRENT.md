## Targets and evaluation

The principal winner target is home win conditional on a non-tied result. Home and away score means, margins, totals and score distributions are distinct tasks. Raw MARKET moneyline consensus is not a learned score distribution. A point forecast does not establish cover/over probability or joint dependence.

Historical evaluation uses separate 2024–2025 and 2025–2026 campaigns, with fixed and annually retrained models identified separately. Historical development has been repeatedly inspected. The 2026–2027 campaign is monitoring only and never selects or ranks a serving policy. Predeclared online updates require their own protocol.

Proper scores, calibration, pick counts and weekly-block uncertainty assess different properties. A negative interaction contrast is evidence of complementarity, not sufficient evidence of superiority over the strongest full model or market control.
