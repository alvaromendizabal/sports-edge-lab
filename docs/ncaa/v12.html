How to read these scores

Accuracy is the share of correct winner selections. Log loss and Brier score measure probability quality; lower is better. Margin and total errors use points: MAE is the average absolute error, while RMSE gives larger misses more weight. These are descriptive scores on the supplied settled games, with incomplete source clocks and coverage still disclosed. They are not a record of predictions actually issued before those games. The scored rows cover 578 of 791 observed completed men’s games (73.1%) and 585 of 790 women’s games (74.1%); 213 men’s and 205 women’s completed games have no score from this model.

Do not compare the current rows with historical scores below as if their seasons, models and game cohorts were interchangeable. A fitted model can forecast later games only from information available by their cutoff. Later reconstructions remain separately labelled.
