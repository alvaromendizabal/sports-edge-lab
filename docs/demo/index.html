The current decision

Keep the accepted baselines.

The historical quarterback experiment produced a modest total-point improvement, but most of it was already present in the continuity control. Development gates failed and uncertainty still included no improvement.

NFL V047 remains the 16-feature team baseline.
NCAAF V048R1 remains the 60-feature team/drive baseline.
