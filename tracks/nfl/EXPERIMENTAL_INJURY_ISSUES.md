# A separate experimental issue series

The dashboard offers **Experimental injury challenger · Nov 4** alongside the preserved forecast streams. It contains 29 genuinely issued early cards: 15 Week 9 games and 14 Week 10 games. These are 87 winner/margin/total target rows, not 87 games or bets. The default forecast and historical scorecards remain unchanged.

Choose **Inspect the experimental Week 9 cards** in the full-season explorer, or select the challenger in **Model forecast stream**. Other streams remain available for direct inspection. The challenger is deliberately absent from historical performance selectors because this addendum does not introduce a historical evaluation table.

## What these models use

The selected winner model uses base features without direct injury fields. The selected margin and total models use injury features and explicit flags for unknown reports. Separate target models can disagree about the favorite; a winner probability and a mean margin answer different statistical questions.

Only Jacksonville–Baltimore has admitted current listed reports in this issue: **nine Baltimore rows and five Jacksonville rows**. Fourteen rows were excluded during identity matching, including Tyler Huntley and Joe Fagnano. Lamar Jackson's listed DNP was included. The pipeline did **not** verify the announced starter, depth chart, full quarterback room or injured-reserve status as model inputs. The other 28 games have no admitted current report rows; that does not mean the players were healthy.

The Jacksonville card projects a **64.2% Jacksonville win probability, Jacksonville by 4.5 points, and 46.9 combined points**. The winner probability is from the base-feature target. This is not a forecast that fully incorporates Baltimore's quarterback situation, and it is not an accepted betting recommendation. The separate official-news context remains visible without being retroactively inserted into the model.

## Authentication and limits

An independent private review fully streamed and checked the six selected native/calibration bodies and three selected fit receipts, including the 87 issued-row bindings and source/fit/issue clocks. This does not authenticate all private study bodies anew or confer full pipeline acceptance. The actual N037 source return remained partial, despite its completed native study.

The public validator checks the sanitized card schema, exact evidence identity, event/team binding, probability/score rounding, early clocks, target-specific injury use and missing-report qualifications. Raw reports, model bodies, tuned recipes and private storage locations are excluded. Earlier JSON exports are unchanged; selecting this series cannot rewrite an original forecast, choose a new recipe using current-season outcomes, or establish an edge or profit.
