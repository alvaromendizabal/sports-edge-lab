## Make conditional metadata agree with the predicate

N048's public cards already use the correctly oriented observed-predicate probabilities. The previously documented generic conditional field is defective for 44 lower-oriented current rows. That defect remains part of the dated N048 record.

The new generic contract calculates both the raw and non-equality conditional probability from the same oriented numerator. For a less-than predicate, the numerator is the probability below the threshold. Strict contracts retain equality as a loss; a void-at-equality contract conditions on the non-equality mass. When all mass is at equality, the conditional probability is unavailable. Unknown settlement can retain descriptive probabilities but cannot supply a scoring probability or an executable-quote claim. This is a proposed owner metadata repair, not a claim that earlier records were rewritten.
