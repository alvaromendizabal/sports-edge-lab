Its checks reject inflated fit accounting, later training labels, issue times at or after kickoff, missing output rows and promotion claims. The export builder writes full CSV and JSON records, with PDF and XLSX companions built from the same enriched projection.

The public overlay is a sanitized projection of authenticated owner outputs. It excludes private model bodies and private storage references. It adds no source-only predictive credit. Full-precision metrics and all six complete paired-comparison records are included in the exports; no proper score is recomputed from rounded display predictions.
