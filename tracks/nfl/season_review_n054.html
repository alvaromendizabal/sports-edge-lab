V5 methodology · prepared implementation

Refresh the evidence. Refit when inputs change.

1. Select using earlier seasons.

Compare recent two-season, three-season, longer, expanding and recency-weighted policies through nested earlier-origin checks. Historical windows are candidates, not a permanent preference. Preserve the original fixed selector as a dated control.

2. Refit at each admissible cutoff.

Make the full verified history available. Each registered candidate selects or weights its own eligible fitting memory, including eligible earlier current-season observations. Transformations learn only from that training data. Reuse exact compatible states.

3. Keep real issues immutable.

Meta layers and adaptive gates use strictly earlier out-of-fold or prequential evidence. Source refresh, refitting, champion selection and evaluation exposure remain separate. Examined replay results stay development-exposed.

The next V5 phase remains shadow research until registered prior-origin comparisons and forward promotion evidence exist. Historical champions remain visible while the current-regime challenger is evaluated.
