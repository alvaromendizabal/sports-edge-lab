Why do 4 and 137 both appear for DraftKings?

For the 149 completed 2026 games, a DraftKings winner quote existed before the two-hour cutoff in all 149. Allowing quotes up to six hours old gave 138 eligible contracts. One tied game refunded the binary winner bet, leaving 137 scored winners. On the same horizon, the stricter V12 rule found five source snapshots no more than five minutes old. Only four also had a native winner quote no more than five minutes old. The five-minute count answers whether a new pregame feature has fresh input at that issue time.

Can we measure prices after a drive or at halftime?

In the broad-source sample, 35 DraftKings provider fixture IDs had a native timestamp at or after scheduled kickoff. The N115 market study found no positively identified after-kickoff quote among its 44,383 grade rows. Each result describes its own collection.

The drive archive includes 4,555 historical drives across 211 games. A drive-reaction feature needs ordered play or possession IDs, game-clock and wall-clock alignment, the matching contract, and fresh quotes before and after that event. Those links have not been verified across the full NFL seasons. A timestamp after the scheduled start alone cannot distinguish a delayed kickoff, a first-quarter drive, halftime or postgame.

Pregame forecasts may use verified target-game prices observed before their declared issue cutoff. Historical drive and market reactions can become features for a later game once the earlier game has finished and the source was available.
