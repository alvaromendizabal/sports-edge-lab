How to read this

The definitions that make a Circa comparison meaningful.

01 · Winner

Who wins the game?

My model predicts a home-win probability. A call counts as correct when the team actually wins, regardless of the points spread. Tied games aren't scored as winner calls. Brier and log loss measure probability accuracy.

02 · Against the spread

Who covers Circa's line?

Circa assigns a fixed line to each side (e.g. Rams −4, Washington +4). The model's projected margin plus the home team's spread determines which side it prefers. If the actual margin lands exactly on the line, it's a push.

03 · Points total

How many points?

My model also estimates combined points, compared with the actual final total (MAE = average absolute error). Circa Million doesn't use over/unders. Only eight Week 9 games have independently documented Saturday cross-book totals; those are marked separately.

04 · Contest format

Five games per week

Circa Million VIII contestants choose five NFL sides weekly from posted fixed spreads. Wins count 1, pushes 0.5, losses 0. Registration for 2026 closed Sept. 12. A five-strongest-edges hindsight card is a comparison tool, not an entered ticket.

05 · Weeks 1–8

Reconstructed, not posted

Historical predictions were recreated in November using the existing model protocol. Training-label cutoffs and OOF model design are documented, but historical feature-source availability is not fully authenticated. No claim of true pregame picks is made.

06 · Week 9

Issued before kickoff, after deadline

Original N056 owner outputs were saved Saturday Nov. 7 at about 11:32 p.m. Pacific, before Sunday's games, but Circa selections were due at 4 p.m. They are valid as issued model forecasts, not Circa-eligible contest entries. Archived injury source-vintage limitations still apply.
