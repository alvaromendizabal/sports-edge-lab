This NFL dashboard reports the saved N091 owner run, 2024–2026 same-cohort comparisons, the 2026 schedule and the captured Week 10 official contest board. The owner recorded 24 new fits, 99 reused fit jobs and 33 failed scoring jobs. It produced 888 qualified upcoming forecast rows across 111 games and eight streams. Production calibration and recorded contest entry remain unavailable. The source-preparation receipt contains the exact asset digests. Deployment evidence is stored separately.
