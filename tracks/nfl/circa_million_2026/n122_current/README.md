# Circa Million Picks & Performance Dashboard

[Open the N122 dashboard](https://sports-edge-lab-nfl.alv-mendizabal.chatgpt.site/circa/n122/) or download index.html and open it in a modern browser. Its interactive tables work offline. The individual CSVs and report can be saved next to the HTML for offline downloads.

- [Full-season model metrics](N122_FULL_SEASON_MODELS.csv) cover all 285 final games in each of 2024 and 2025 and all 148 completed games in the 2026 test. The public decision table uses only the two validation years to select nominees.
- [Every NFL game and its venue coverage](N122_GAME_VENUE_COVERAGE.csv) keeps all 842 games, including the live game and scheduled games with unconfirmed kickoffs. Indicators distinguish observation and exact product matching.
- [Same-contract commercial comparisons](N122_MATCHED_VENUE_PROBABILITY.csv) and [dated weekly market comparison](N122_WEEKLY_MARKET_SCORES.csv) document their respective clocks, settlement rules and eligible cases.
- [Projection table](N122_FORWARD_PROJECTIONS.csv) distinguishes published N121 research, N122 pregame research, a started-game guard, and restored season-opening models.
- [Circa five-pick cards](N122_CIRCA_CARDS.csv) and [all eligible sides](N122_CIRCA_ALTERNATIVES.csv) record 46 historical contest worksheets, prior ATS records, cover and push probabilities and known settlements.
- [Read the N122 debrief](N122_Full_Season_Owner_Debrief.pdf) for nominations, uncertainty, signed forecasts, historical model recovery and remaining gaps.
