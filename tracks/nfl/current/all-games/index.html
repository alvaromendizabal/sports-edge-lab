How to read the scores

A win earns one point, a push earns half and a loss earns zero. Each policy picks one side per game and ranks games by P(cover) + ½ × P(push). The contest uses five distinct games each week. All-games and remaining-games grades are research comparisons.

Uniform five is the expected realized score from choosing five of that policy’s sides uniformly within each week. For each week, multiply its all-game points by five and divide by its number of games, then sum over weeks. Lift is actual top-five points minus that benchmark. Week 12 expected points are forward estimates.
