How the replay was scored

N209 selected these candidates on the exposed 2026 results. For this replay, the saved 2024 components used only earlier seasons. The saved 2025 components could use 2024. The historical features stayed frozen. These picks were reconstructed after the games.

The 26 playoff games lacked market lines in the original model inputs. The replay used 52 archived DraftKings spread and total quote pairs. Each quote was observed before the lock one hour before the first kickoff of its playoff round. Prices defined the grading line or the strike queried by the direct spread model.

6 roles shared 5 fitted candidates. The replay newly scored 52 game–model pairs and verified 78 reusable grades. That fills the postseason gap for the selected models. The original annual comparison across all candidates still covers the regular-season rows with prices.
