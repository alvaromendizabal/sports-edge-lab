Coverage and comparison rules

Each season, phase, target, horizon and evidence track has one required game membership. Every required candidate remains visible, including candidates with zero qualified predictions. Complete scores require the same membership fingerprint and every required game. A common intersection is a diagnostic only and cannot become the headline denominator.

Candidate selection belongs to the two validation seasons. The current test season has already been examined in prior analysis, so it is an exposed test rather than an untouched confirmation set. Its scores cannot be used to tune a candidate and then be relabeled independent test evidence. Later-fitted reconstructions remain separate from valid replay and original-issued forecasts. Historical champions stay preserved; no model is automatically promoted.

A provider comparison has additional game, target, line or contract, quote-clock, fee, liquidity and settlement checks. A model-only full-population score does not establish complete market coverage. A paid acquisition receipt does not establish a qualified benchmark.

What goes into a valid comparison?

Separate the prediction targets

Winner models estimate who wins. Margin models estimate the point difference. Total models estimate combined scoring. A win probability cannot become a spread-cover probability without an exact line and a validated score distribution.

Use the right information clock

Team form, possessions, efficiency, shots, rest and lineups must come from evidence available before the target game's cutoff. Historical sequences can describe earlier completed games; target-game halftime or final statistics cannot enter a pregame forecast.

Match source and model evidence

The game, team orientation, target, line or contract, quote time and settlement rule must agree. US and international Polymarket products stay separate. Missing quote clocks do not inherit the retrieval time.

Keep the missing denominator

Unscheduled season slots, unresolved matchups, unavailable forecasts and unqualified market references remain gaps. No data is treated as a zero value, and a small matched sample cannot stand in for the whole season.
