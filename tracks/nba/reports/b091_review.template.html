What happens before a champion decision

Complete the evidence

  • Recover exact authentic model states and preserve original weights.
  • Bind source-compliant feature producers and training populations.
  • Fill eligible model coverage against the unchanged full population.
  • Keep historical reconstructions separate from actual issued forecasts.

Keep the decision honest

  • Select nominees on the two validation seasons.
  • Use current test outcomes only to evaluate those frozen choices.
  • Label changed recipes and split policies as challengers.
  • Retain uncertainty, ties, missing evidence and negative results.
