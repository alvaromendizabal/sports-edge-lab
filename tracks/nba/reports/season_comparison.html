What goes into a valid comparison?

Separate the prediction targets

Winner models estimate who wins. Margin models estimate the point difference. Total models estimate combined scoring. A win probability cannot become a spread-cover probability without an exact line and a validated score distribution.

Use the right information clock

Team form, possessions, efficiency, shots, rest and lineups must come from evidence available before the target game's cutoff. Historical sequences can describe earlier completed games; target-game halftime or final statistics cannot enter a pregame forecast.

Match source and model evidence

The game, team orientation, target, line or contract, quote time and settlement rule must agree. US and international Polymarket products stay separate. Missing quote clocks do not inherit the retrieval time.

Keep the missing denominator

Unscheduled season slots, unresolved matchups, unavailable forecasts and unqualified market references remain gaps. No data is treated as a zero value, and a small matched sample cannot stand in for the whole season.
