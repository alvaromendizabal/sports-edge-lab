Know what a number means

Read the clocks with the forecasts

Saved issue versus reconstruction

Per-game numbers appear only when the selected role has a saved pregame issue time. A reconstruction with no pregame issue remains unavailable here. Historical aggregates retain their exposed replay qualifications. A completed game can still show its saved early outlook.

Current-source application versus retained issue

The winner and margin role may reference the refreshed population while preserving an earlier original issue. The total role currently retains its older saved outlook. These are separate clocks; publication does not refresh any of them.

Signed margin and probability

Signed margin means away score minus home score. Positive favors the away team; negative favors the home team. Winner probability comes from its separate selected probability role and can disagree with the margin role. Probabilities are model estimates, with calibration qualification still incomplete.

Two Kalshi settlement sides

YES and NO are complementary under normal contract settlement. Equality follows the exact wording. Cancellation, void and pending cases stay separate. No tie or push selection, invented half-point, or half-credit is used for Kalshi.

Selection stays outside the test season

Each objective is selected using an equal average of its 2024–25 and 2025–26 validation metrics. Sensitivity ratios reuse the same predictions. Previously inspected 2026–27 results cannot become pristine retrospectively and cannot choose a model, strike or blend.

A snapshot, not a deployed model

This page publishes a sanitized view of saved research. It is not a live quote feed or a wagering interface. A public website, research completion, model promotion and provider retrieval are separately recorded events. The future schedule remains provisional where official scheduling is unreleased.
