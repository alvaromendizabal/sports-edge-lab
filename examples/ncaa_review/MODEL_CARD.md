## Intended use and populations

The project produces analytical winner probabilities, point margins and total-point estimates for NCAA men’s and women’s basketball. The populations, source clocks, target definitions and evaluation memberships remain separate. Descriptive results and source observations cannot substitute for a qualified prediction.

## Research and champion selection

Frozen completed-season research retains all **10 registered model families and 26 target heads** on **2024–25 and 2025–26**. Evaluation settings and model configurations are fixed before the applicable evaluation. Source availability, earlier completed training labels, target membership and calibration remain explicit requirements. Registered, available, fitted, evaluated and selected are different states.

Seven objective-specific roles select qualified champions:

| Target | Objective | Preferred direction |
|---|---|---|
| Winner | Accuracy | Higher |
| Winner | Log loss | Lower |
| Winner | Brier score | Lower |
| Point margin | MAE | Lower |
| Point margin | RMSE | Lower |
| Total points | MAE | Lower |
| Total points | RMSE | Lower |

The selected recipe and its exact eligible evaluation cohort must remain reviewable. An objective does not borrow a different target’s score or a provider cohort with different membership. The historical configuration registry remains incomplete; this preparation does not establish that every earlier variant has been recovered or retested.

**No 2026–27 outcome-based selection or promotion is permitted.** Missing qualification produces an explicit gap. Current-season outcomes may become earlier completed training information for a later game, subject to their real availability, without selecting a champion on that season’s results.

## Current per-game execution

Current rolling fits are restricted to **qualified native champions and the selected, precommitted components of a champion ensemble**. The full research grid is not retrained for every current game. The selected configurations retrain on all eligible information available before the game cutoff. Exact checkpoint reuse requires its existing source, runtime, input, membership and artifact proofs.

Current-season historical rolling reconstruction uses only the selected native/component recipes. It remains separate from original frozen forecasts, numeric unissued references and genuinely issued future predictions. A reconstructed old game never gains an earlier issue timestamp. Missing source, feature, calibration or forecast evidence remains visible.
