# Repository collaboration contract

Read CONTRIBUTING.md and docs/PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY.md before changes. Preserve existing research and provenance. Inspect current main and open PRs before writing or merging.

## Scope ownership

Track-specific work belongs in its established track or example directory. NFL work must not change FBS, NBA or NCAA modeling results. Shared README, navigation, CI and publication policy changes require one explicit integration owner who reconciles active branches and preserves other tracks' dated evidence.

## Public/private boundary

Only publish sanitized aggregates, the explicitly permitted resolved examples, selected rounded early NFL research cards, the authorized FBS all-game analytical report, generic engineering implementations and invented fixtures. The FBS extension is defined in docs/PUBLICATION_BOUNDARY.md and does not expand other tracks. Never publish owner PYZ/return/continuation packages, credentials, private cloud paths, licensed feed arrays, active model weights or exact private feature transforms. Keep private outreach recipients and recruiting conversations out of all public content.

## Scientific and merge checks

Retain original/rolling/issued distinctions, event/horizon/target/settlement matching, information clocks, missing-coverage labels and negative findings. Do not claim prospective profit from reconstructed metrics. Run affected tests and render checks, inspect exact-head CI, reconcile concurrent base changes, and merge without bypassing rules or forcing refs. Report local and hosted validation separately.
